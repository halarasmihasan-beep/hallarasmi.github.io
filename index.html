04 — Graduation Project

Antoq

An educational app that helps children with speech difficulties learn, play and practice — out loud.

Antoq is an interactive educational application built in Unity for children with speech difficulties. It pairs learning objectives with playful gameplay, uses Whisper AI-powered speech recognition for pronunciation practice, and layers in Augmented Reality experiences built with Vuforia to keep kids engaged. Progress is saved locally with PlayerPrefs, and every interface was designed to stay accessible and friendly for children with diverse needs.

  • 🗣️ Whisper AI speech recognition for pronunciation practice
  • 🪄 Vuforia-powered Augmented Reality learning experiences
  • 🎯 Educational mini-games tied to real learning objectives
  • 💾 Local progress tracking across sessions with PlayerPrefs
  • 🧩 Accessible, kid-first interface design
